This is a serious claim. It is the kind of claim that can be true or false based on what people inside the organization actually intended and on what their actions revealed. It is empirically anchored — Hao spent years interviewing OpenAI insiders for «Empire of AI», so she has standing to make this read. The counter-position would be that the nonprofit-to-for-profit pivot reflected a real strategic and cultural shift, driven by compute economics making safety-pure research impractical at the frontier. Both readings have evidence behind them. A careful reader should hold the question open, not nod along — Hao is making a stronger version of "OpenAI was never what it claimed to be" than the typical critic, and that's a position worth examining, not ratifying.
